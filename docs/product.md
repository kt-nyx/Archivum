# Product and MVP

Archivum is an in-game guide to the place a player is exploring. A page should explain what the place is, why it matters at the start of the playable story, what happened there, and which related content the player can explore next.

## Product decisions

- **Retail World of Warcraft.** Choose the active playable version as the point of view. Older events can explain history when the evidence establishes the timeline. Place present-day claims at the start of the relevant playable story (entry state), and keep later quest outcomes and encounter reveals out of the setup.
- **Zone and instance pages.** A zone page has an overview at a glance, current state, history, major factions, major questlines, typed location cards, related instances, and reference links. An instance page covers story setup, history, key participants, factions, related quests, and its parent zone. The original design prioritizes dungeons and raids and treats variants as distinct only when their playable stories materially differ.
- **Short guides inside WoW.** Zone and instance pages are the internal navigation targets. Cities, starter areas, and other notable places are cards on their parent page. References use the canonical Warcraft Wiki URL and a copy dialog. The packaged addon reads static data locally. The longer-term design opens the guide from the world map.
- **Questlines as player-facing hooks.** A card represents an important story arc with a clear title, any meaningful faction or phase variant, and an evidence-backed starting quest. Its short invitation to act (CTA, or call to action) explains the premise before the story unfolds.
- **Source-backed selection and writing.** Use page ownership, categories, links, quest structure, and encounter membership to identify relevant subjects. Link each important claim back to evidence and record why a candidate was included or dropped. Pilot inputs may be named in evaluation manifests and historical examples. Shared selection, prompts, and validation must work across zones without a preset editorial answer.

## Recommended path to the first playable build

1. **Re-establish a live baseline.** Run Westfall and Deadmines first on the current tree. Westfall was held out while building the recovery and exposed both missing coverage and weakly grounded prose. Save the run under a new ID and audit a small set of flagged fields against their exact citations. Distinguish evidence that was never fetched, evidence that was fetched but omitted or cited incorrectly, prose that added a claim, and an overly strict checker verdict. This identifies the earliest fix for each example.
2. **Close the failures with shared changes.** Follow the worst claims back through selection, writing, and citation resolution. Make the writer revise or omit unsupported claims and verify the validator still accepts supported paraphrases. For Westfall’s missing locations, check which strongly evidenced places were queued after the crawl limit and prioritize that signal generically. Fix leaked or doubled title suffixes and section-description text used as quest hooks where the current replay reproduces them. Keep fixtures small and check the same fixes on Desolace and Western Plaguelands.
3. **Build one playable vertical slice.** A vertical slice is one complete path from generated data to what a player sees. As soon as a representative bundle exists, connect one zone and its instance to the addon using a fixture for the interface work if necessary. Add a scrollable page, zone-to-instance and back navigation, and canonical wiki links. Test the package on the supported Retail client and confirm it loads without a network connection. The slash command is sufficient for this first milestone.
4. **Prepare the MVP.** Aim for a short, reviewed catalog of two distinct zone/instance pairs, with a third pair as a generalization check. Each supported pair needs a strict validation pass and a shared editorial review. Package the approved data, check the Retail interface version in the addon manifest, finish attribution and distribution notices, and manually smoke-test the install. Map entry, more zones, progress tracking, and broader distribution can follow.

The MVP catalog size, ordering, and slash-command milestone are recommendations for restarting work. See [project status](status.md) for the evidence behind them.
