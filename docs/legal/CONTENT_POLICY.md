# Content Policy

- Write original zone and instance prose from the evidence supplied for that field. Support entities, relationships, and timeline claims with traceable sources.
- Give players spoiler-light quest hooks and starting guidance. Keep quest and encounter outcomes beyond the playable entry point out of the setup.
- Apply firm retail and entity-type exclusions before drafting. Location candidates with hard exclusions, such as removed or other-game locations, stay excluded.
- Use language models for evidence-backed prose and bounded judgment calls where source signals are ambiguous. Canonical IDs, hard exclusions, and source ownership come from the pipeline's evidence and contracts.

See [product decisions](../product.md#product-decisions) for the page structure and [evaluation](../../evaluation/README.md) for the editorial review.
