# Content Policy

## Writing Constraints

- Do not fabricate entities, relationships, or timeline claims.
- Zone/instance prose must be generated only from bounded evidence packs.
- Questline output uses CTA-style hooks; spoiler-heavy story beat lists are disallowed.
- Hard-rejected location candidates (`undisplayed`, `lore`, `removed`, `other-game`) cannot be promoted by LLM adjudication.

## LLM Responsibility Boundary

- Allowed: concise player-facing prose and borderline significance/inclusion adjudication.
- Disallowed: source override, canonical ID changes, or bypassing hard-reject decisions.
